Threats to Our Forests

Hover or tap a card to flip it.

🪓

Deforestation

Forests are cleared for farming, mining and timber, destroying habitats in seconds that took centuries to grow.

🔥

Wildfires

Climate change and human carelessness cause massive fires that release carbon and wipe out wildlife.

🏭

Pollution

Acid rain and industrial waste poison soil and water, weakening trees and the life that depends on them.

🌡️

Climate Change

Rising temperatures bring droughts, pests and disease that stress forests beyond their ability to recover.

How You Can Help

🌱

Plant Trees

Plant native trees in your neighbourhood, school or farm. One tree can absorb about 22 kg of CO₂ a year.

♻️

Reduce & Recycle

Use less paper, recycle, and choose products with sustainable forestry certification.

📢

Spread Awareness

Talk to friends, share on social media and support laws that protect forests.

🤝

Support NGOs

Donate or volunteer with organisations that restore forests and protect wildlife.
